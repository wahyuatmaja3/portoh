04 Process

How I work

The same loop on every project, whether it ships in a week or gets maintained for years.

  • 01

    Read the room

    I start by finding the real constraint. Who uses this, what breaks today, and what "better" actually means for the person on the other side of the screen.

  • 02

    Cut the scope

    The first version is the smallest thing that can honestly answer the brief. Everything else gets parked, not quietly buried in the backlog.

  • 03

    Build in the open

    Short cycles, something running early, and a demo before the feature is finished. Problems are far cheaper to find on a working build.

  • 04

    Measure, then trim

    I look at what people actually touch and take the friction out of it. Screens get smaller, code gets shorter, and both read better.

  • 05

    Keep it standing

    Launch is the midpoint, not the finish line. I stay on the apps I ship, including the older desktop systems that still need care.
